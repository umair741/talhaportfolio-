Designing Physical Spaces & Visual Brands

I am a visual storyteller specializing in translating conceptual design requirements into detailed, realistic 3D spaces and promotional designs. My background is rooted in creating high-impact trade show booths, custom exhibition kiosks, canopy tents, roll-up banners, and premium product renders.

My process combines accurate spatial proportions, realistic lighting, and clean material textures to ensure that physical brand assets translate perfectly onto digital presentations and event setups. I work closely with agencies and direct clients globally to execute clean, striking, and functional concepts.
